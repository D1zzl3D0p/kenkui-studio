# Existing Kenkui audio previews

These recordings were copied from `kenkui-site/public/audio` and the previously approved Studio prototype. No new speech was generated for this implementation. `src/studio/voice-catalog.json` preserves the site's voice IDs, sample text, and attribution metadata.

Voice samples are derived from the CSTR VCTK Corpus via Kyutai/Kenkui, under CC BY 4.0. The voice picker displays attribution and license links:

- Corpus: https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/3443
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Selected voice clips and the two Pride and Prejudice scene clips have PCM WAV copies for browser codec compatibility. These are format conversions of the existing recordings. Scene clips demonstrate narration style and do not represent the user's uploaded text or chosen voices.
